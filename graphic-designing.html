FAQ

Design FAQ

Quick answers to typical queries about our graphic design services.

What file formats do you deliver?

We provide all standard formats including PNG, JPG, PDF, and source files like PSD, AI, or Figma links depending on the project requirements.

Do you offer unlimited revisions?

We typically include a set number of revisions (usually 2-3 rounds) to ensure the project stays on track, but we always work with you until you are completely satisfied.

Can you follow our existing brand guidelines?

Absolutely! We ensure all new designs are perfectly aligned with your existing brand identity, typography, and color schemes.
